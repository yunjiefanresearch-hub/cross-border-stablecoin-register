# Qualitative codebook and audit protocol

## Research question

How do stablecoin rules change the legal feasibility of a directed cross-border action, and which
conclusion is supported by operative, current, primary-source evidence rather than policy, proposal or
market fact?

## Unit of analysis

One jurisdiction × regulatory dimension × instrument record. Corridor conclusions are derived units and
must retain links to the underlying records; they are never independently coded as legal facts.

## Codes

| Code | Apply when | Do not apply when |
|---|---|---|
| `operative_positive_rule` | enacted rule is in force and permits or requires conduct | proposal, not commenced, no regime |
| `operative_prohibition` | operative authority forecloses the action/path | absence of evidence alone |
| `enacted_not_commenced` | law is made/promulgated but operative date has not arrived or is unset | bill passed one chamber only |
| `proposal_not_law` | consultation, NPRM or bill remains non-final | final rule merely awaits future effective date |
| `authority_split` | two authorities have distinct statutory roles | two sources merely restate the same rule |
| `cross_border_gate` | equivalence, recognition, marketing, location or currency condition affects inbound/outbound use | domestic-only prudential rule with no corridor mechanism |
| `operational_fact` | licence, launch, registration or market availability | proposition about the legal perimeter |
| `source_conflict` | authoritative texts materially disagree or a summary contradicts the operative text | different detail levels without contradiction |
| `freshness_risk` | review is due, stale or unknown under the published SLA | current source check with mapped pinpoint |

## Coding workflow

1. Capture official URL, publication date, instrument identity and the exact operative pinpoint.
2. Separate enactment, commencement, effective date, transitional period and implementation guidance.
3. Code claim kind, evidence tier and binding status independently.
4. Run the schema and contradiction gates.
5. A second reviewer repeats the source read without seeing the first disposition.
6. Reconcile disagreements in a decision log; only then promote freshness or evidence tier.

## Pilot status

The 20 August 2026 targeted pilot covers four high-priority official-source leads (TW, US, UK, HK).
It was single-coded. Inter-rater reliability is therefore **not calculated**; no fabricated Cohen's kappa
or agreement rate is reported. The full 152-record pass and an independent legal reviewer remain release
gates.
